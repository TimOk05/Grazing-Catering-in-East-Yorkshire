Good to know

Before you
send a message

Which areas do you cover?+

Gather & Graze Yorkshire is registered in HU10, East Riding of Yorkshire. Send the venue postcode with your enquiry so the team can confirm availability for your location.

Can dietary requirements be considered?+

Include allergies and dietary requirements in your first message. The team can then explain what can be accommodated for your chosen format.

How far ahead should I enquire?+

Availability varies by date and event size, so it is always best to ask as soon as your date and venue are taking shape.

What details should I include?+

Share your preferred date, venue postcode, approximate guest count, type of occasion and the service you are interested in.
